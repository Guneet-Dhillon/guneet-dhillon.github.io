I completed my DPhil in Statistics at the University of Oxford (2021&ndash;26), where my research focused on the robustness of modern predictive and generative machine learning systems.
I was supervised by Yee Whye Teh, Tom Rainforth, George Deligiannidis, and Arnaud Doucet, and was also awarded the Clarendon Fund Scholarship.

Before my DPhil, I spent three years as an Applied Scientist at Amazon Web Services (2018&ndash;21).
I received a BSc in Computer Science (Honors and Turing Scholars Honors) and a BSc in Mathematics (Honors) from the University of Texas at Austin (2014&ndash;18), where I also completed a certificate in Applied Statistical Modeling, a minor in Economics, and an honors thesis supervised by Adam Klivans.
